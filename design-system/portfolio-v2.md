# Portfolio v2 design contract

Approved direction: a light editorial engineering portfolio for recruiters and engineering teams assessing Werkstudent and Master's thesis candidates.

- Off-white #f7f6f2, charcoal #182334, cobalt #2458d3; white project surfaces and a navy contact section.
- Keep the existing locally bundled Manrope font. Hierarchy comes from size, weight, spacing and grid alignment.
- Compact identity-led hero, primary Locate-IQ showcase followed by RTLS and analytics, experience timeline, additional project gallery, About and contact.
- Reuse existing content, imagery, shareable dialogs, sample controls and English/German CVs. Keep employer-work and synthetic-data labels visible.
- Native links, buttons, forms and dialogs; visible keyboard focus, 44px controls, reduced-motion support and content reflow down to 320px.
- Verify lint, unit tests, production build, browser interactions, image/CV loading, layouts, 200% text size and contrast. Deploy to the existing anshaj Vercel project after verification.

UI/UX Pro Max v2.0 recommended Minimalism & Swiss Style, a light monochrome palette with a blue accent, and a clear grid. The approved direction adapts that guidance to the existing engineering content and bundled font. No new runtime dependencies.
